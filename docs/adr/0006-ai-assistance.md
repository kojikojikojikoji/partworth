# ADR-0006: Use of AI assistance

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-12

Parts of partworth's code, tests and documentation were drafted with an AI coding
assistant. The model specification, priors, validation targets and the scope of each
release are the author's decisions and are recorded in these ADRs. Every change is
covered by tests, and statistical behaviour is checked by parameter-recovery tests and
the comparison with R bayesm in `validation/`.
